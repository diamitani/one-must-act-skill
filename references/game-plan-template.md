## The equation

```
0  = where we are today (measured, not imagined)
1  = a working platform that can scale (whole and complete)
2–99 = the multipliers: partners, customers, team, investors, vendors, family, friends, God
100 = the definite goal
```

**The rule: you cannot multiply a fraction.** Nothing gets multiplied (no paid
acquisition, no fundraising push, no hiring spree) until the venture is a true **1**.
Once it is 1, every multiplier added must be chosen on purpose, because each one
carries a share of the distance to 100.

The order of work:

1. Pick the end goal: definite and specific (section 1).
2. Define the 1: what has to exist for the goal to be possible (section 3).
3. Pick the multipliers you'll add once the 1 exists, and when (section 5).
4. Start from 0, today (sections 2 and 4).
